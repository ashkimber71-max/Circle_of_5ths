Choose a key in the inner ring. The window frames the chords that belong to it, and the outer ring tells you its sharps or flats.

    Look at the key signature and name the major key. Turn the card to check, then sort it: cards you're still learning come back round.

    Space turns the card. Then press 1 for still learning or 2 for got it.

    Name the triad on the stave. Start with one key, then work up to inversions and every triad type.

    Press 1–4 to answer, Enter for the next triad, H for help and K for the piano.

    You'll hear chord I, the home chord, followed by a second chord from the same key. Which chord of the key is the second one? Turn your sound on first.

    Press P to play, 2–7 to answer and Enter for the next pair.

    Modes are scales made from the notes of another scale, usually the major scale, starting on a different note. Starting on a new note changes where the tones and semitones fall, which gives each mode its own mood.

    Press 1–7 to choose a mode, P to play it, D to play it over a drone, M to hear it in a melody and C to compare.
